Session date: 2026-06-03 · Branch: DS-CNN_Optimization_Iker

1 — Root Bug Discovered

The deployed ds_cnn_l_static_edgetpu.tflite was actually a 4-block model, not 6-block.
convert_static_int8.py was loading training/trained_models/kws_ref_model (the MLCommons 4-block DS-CNN, ~2.6M MACs) instead of the 6-block DS-CNN-L (~3.94M MACs). It also defaulted to random calibration data.
