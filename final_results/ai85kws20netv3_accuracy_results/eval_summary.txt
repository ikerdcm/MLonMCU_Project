ai85kws20netv3 — Evaluation Results
========================================================
Dataset:   KWS_20 (21 classes: 20 keywords + unknown)
           Test set: 11,005 samples
Device:    MAX78000 (simulate=True, no GPU)
Framework: ai8x-training

Model                         Accuracy   Correct/Total   Weights
--------------------------------------------------------------
Baseline — INT8 QAT           90.80%    9993 / 11005    ~668 KB (unpruned)
w90 + QAT — INT8 pruned       85.10%    9364 / 11005    ~145 KB (-78%)
Float32 CPU (no CNN accel.)   91.17%   10033 / 11005    ~2.3 MB (float32)

Pruning loss (INT8): -5.70 percentage points (90% structured pruning)
Weight reduction (INT8): 668 KB → 145 KB  (-78.3%)

Float32 note: Weights extracted from ai85-kws20_v3-qat8.pth.tar (QAT float32,
              no post-training quantization). Inference runs on Cortex-M4F FPU
              in software (no CNN hardware accelerator). Accuracy is 0.37 pp
              higher than INT8 baseline due to no quantization rounding error.

Note: Paper reports 96.3% for 12-class variant (different task).
      These results use 21-class KWS_20 (harder problem).
